Two clocks run around a challenge, and both are configurable per site.

| Setting | What it bounds | Default |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| **Solution timeout** | From the challenge being issued to the user submitting an answer | 60 seconds |
| **Verified timeout** | From the user solving to your server calling verify | 120 seconds |

Both accept 1 to 600 seconds.

<Image src={CaptchaTimeouts} alt="prosopo portal CAPTCHA timeout settings" style="margin: 20px auto;"/>

## Solution timeout

How long the user has to solve the challenge in front of them. A solution submitted after this window is rejected as
stale and the user gets a fresh challenge.

Raise it if your challenge sits on a page where people are legitimately slow — a long form they fill in around the
widget, or an accessibility need. Lower it and you shorten the window in which a challenge can be farmed out to a
solving service, at the cost of failing genuine slow users.

## Verified timeout

How long your server has, after the user solves, to call
[server-side verification](/en/basics/server-side-verification/) with the token. Past this window the token is no longer
verifiable and your call returns unverified.

This is the one to think about if your form does work between the user's click and your verify call — a slow upload, a
payment step, a queue. The window has to cover your worst case, not your median. Shorter windows reduce the time a
leaked or shared token is worth anything to someone who intercepts it.

:::caution
The verified timeout is the one that fails silently in production. It is measured against your server's clock reaching
Prosopo, so it has to cover your slowest path — retries, cold starts, a queued job between submit and verify — not your
average one. Set below that and the symptom is genuine users failing verification with a valid token.
:::

## Where to set them

The **Captcha Timeouts** card is on the **General Settings** tab of Site Settings. Changes apply to new sessions on that
site key; nothing needs redeploying.
