These pages report what [Protect](/en/portal/protect/) did with your traffic. Each has a **Window** from the last 5 minutes to the last 30 days.

## Overview

**Protect → Overview** shows request totals with breakdowns of **Why blocked** and **Why challenged**. A **Partial window** notice means the figures cover less than the window you chose: either recorded traffic starts after the window begins, or the latest figures haven't been rolled up yet.

From here you can drill into:

- **Top Blocked IPs**: IPs with the most blocks, filterable by **Blocked By** (bot detection, access rules, IP intelligence, protocol, captcha outcome or Android Play Integrity), with country, ASN and VPN/Tor/proxy flags.
- **Top Detection Rules**: the heuristic and ML rules that fired most often.

## Challenge Outcomes

Answers "are real people getting through?". It shows how many visitors were **Challenged**, how many **Attempted a captcha**, **Solved at least one**, **Never solved one** or have **No recorded attempt**.

## Traffic Trends

Charts verdicts over time. Use **Filter A** and **Filter B** to **Compare** two slices, **Stack by** a field, limit to the **Top N series**, and set **Auto-refresh**. Click a series chip to hide or show it.
