**Get Started** walks you through integrating Procaptcha in three steps:

1. **Choose an integration**: a WordPress plugin (Contact Form 7, WPForms, WooCommerce and others) or a direct integration.
2. **Add your site key**: copy the site key and secret from [Site Management](/en/portal/sites/).
3. **Test and ship**.

The page also links to [Settings](/en/portal/settings/), these docs and Discord.

See [Client-side rendering](/en/basics/client-side-rendering/) and [Server-side verification](/en/basics/server-side-verification/) for the integration itself.
