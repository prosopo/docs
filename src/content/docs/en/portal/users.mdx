title: User Management
description: Invite team members and set their roles.
i18nReady: true
---

**User Management** lists everyone with access to your account.

- **Add User**: enter their email address and name, and pick a role.
- **Edit** or **Remove** an existing user.

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Admin | Can view and change everything |
| Viewer | Read-only: can view pages but not change settings, rules or policies |
