**Traffic** charts CAPTCHA activity for the selected site:

- **Total Traffic** and **Sessions**.
- Traffic per CAPTCHA type: **PoW**, **Image**, **Puzzle**, **Icon Order** and **Audio**.
- **Global Traffic Distribution**: requests by country.
- **Traffic Filter Breakdown**: how many requests the [traffic filter](/en/advanced/traffic-filter/) caught, by category.
