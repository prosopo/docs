**Agent Trust** sets how [Protect](/en/portal/protect/) treats AI agents and crawlers. It appears when the feature is enabled for your account.

## Known AI agents

Each known agent is listed with its **Provider** and **Type**. Set its **Policy** to **Allow**, **Challenge** or **Block**.

## Custom verifiers

Add your own agents by **Name** and **Signature-Agent URL**. Requests signed with [Web Bot Auth](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/webbotauth/about/) from that URL are matched to the verifier.

Users with the Viewer role can see policies and verifiers but cannot change them.
