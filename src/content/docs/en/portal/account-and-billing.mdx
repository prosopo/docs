i18nReady: true

Your current plan is shown as **Current Tier** on the [Overview](/en/portal/overview/).

- **Upgrade Plan** opens the plan choice. Paid plans are paid through Stripe.
- **Manage Subscription** opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can change or cancel your plan and update payment details.

Some features, such as extra sites, traffic filtering and the spam filter, need a paid plan. The portal shows an upgrade prompt where they apply. See [pricing](https://prosopo.io/pricing/).
