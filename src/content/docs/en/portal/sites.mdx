title: Site Management
description: Create sites and copy their keys.
i18nReady: true
---

**Site Management** lists your sites. Each card shows the **Site Key**, **Secret Key** and **Allowed Domains**.

- **Create New Site**: enter a site name. Free plans are limited; the portal offers **Upgrade Now** when you reach the limit.
- **Set as Default**: the site the portal selects when you sign in.
- **Edit**: change allowed domains.
- **Remove**: delete a site after confirming.

Use the site key in your [client-side integration](/en/basics/client-side-rendering/) and the secret key for [server-side verification](/en/basics/server-side-verification/).
